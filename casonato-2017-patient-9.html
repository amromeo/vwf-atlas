What to notice

Compare patient 9 with the separately labeled NP reference. The authors place this patient in the group with abnormal multimer patterns and loss of large multimers. Band intensity and the degree of visible loss vary between the pictured patients; interpret this lane with its own laboratory results and assay context.

This is proposed atlas teaching commentary, awaiting scientific review. The gel appearance alone does not establish type 2B VWD.

What the authors report

Figure 1 depicts selected patients from Table 1. Patient 9 belongs to family B and has the reported p.R1308C variant. The source image's embedded antigen and collagen-binding values are linked to the same numbered patient.

Interpretation and limitations

The paper also illustrates source-classified type 2B patients with preserved multimer patterns in Figure 2. Those are different patients; their metadata must not be assigned to this example.

Patient-specific bleeding sites, presentation, treatment at the time of the gel, and the exact sample date are not reported in the inspected case-linked material. A shared family identifier does not establish an individual clinical history. Source NA means not assessed and is retained explicitly.

The study enrolled patients during annual haemostatic-center check-ups. It describes DDAVP experiments, but the timing of this particular gel is not established as post-DDAVP. The exact gel-to-laboratory draw linkage and patient-specific PT/aPTT are not reported in the inspected material.

Source-reported ratios and reference intervals are retained without recalculation. The paper's intervals are not universal diagnostic thresholds; interpretation requires attention to the patient's age and assay. Historical subgroup or treatment proposals are not adopted as current consensus or treatment advice.

03 / Reading the assay

Method & controls

Multimer method
Plasma multimer electrophoresis in 1.8% agarose, using ¹²⁵I-labelled anti-VWF antibody (Figure 1 caption).
Specimen
Blood collected with 3.8% sodium citrate (1/10 vol/vol). Additional inhibitors described for platelet VWF sampling are not assumed to apply to this plasma gel.
Lane identities
Full Figure 1, left to right: NP, 17, 18, NP, 10, 9, NP, 19. This page selects patient 9 (lane 6) and NP lane 7.
Normal reference
NP reference lanes are present in the source figure. Exact normal-pool composition and donor identities are not specified in the inspected caption.
Limits of the record
Additional assay protocol details are referred to an earlier method paper that has not yet been reviewed. The exact draw linkage between gel and laboratory values is not established. A PFA cartridge type is not assigned in this record.
