## What to notice

Compare patient 10 with the adjacent NP reference. The upper multimer ladder is depleted relative to normal plasma. Read this appearance alongside the reported activity results and the authors' patient classification; the gel appearance alone does not establish type 2B VWD.

This is proposed atlas teaching commentary, awaiting scientific review.

## What the authors report

Figure 1 represents patients lacking large VWF multimers. The Results describe a marked reduction in large multimers and changes in intermediate multimers in this group. Patient 10 belongs to family C and carries the reported p.R1308C variant.

## Interpretation and limitations

The study also shows source-classified type 2B patients with preserved multimer patterns in Figure 2. A condition can therefore have more than one illustrated appearance; those are separate patients and their laboratory data do not belong to patient 10.

The study enrolled patients during annual haemostatic-center check-ups. It also describes DDAVP experiments, but this record does not establish a post-DDAVP time point for Figure 1. Individual bleeding sites and an exact specimen date are not available in the inspected patient-linked material.

Reported ratios are retained as published rather than recomputed from rounded assay values. Reference intervals belong to this publication and are not universal diagnostic thresholds. Patient-specific PT and aPTT values are not reported in the inspected material.

Historical subgroup and treatment proposals in the article have not been adopted as current consensus or treatment advice. The teaching interpretation and descriptive labels require the scientific editor's review.
