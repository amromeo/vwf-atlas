# VWF Atlas

A visual teaching and reference atlas of von Willebrand factor multimer patterns for pathology trainees, laboratory professionals, and hematologists.

## Language

**Multimer pattern**:
An observable distribution and structural appearance of VWF multimers, described with its assay context. A pattern may be associated with multiple conditions, and a condition may have more than one appearance.

**Atlas entry**:
A teaching and reference description of a multimer pattern, illustrated by one or more published examples and linked to associated conditions.

**Published example**:
An authentic multimer gel example from an identified publication, including a review article, retained with its source and reported clinical and laboratory context.

**Example context**:
The patient history, associated coagulation study results, assay details, and other relevant information reported for a specific published example. This context belongs to that example rather than to every example of the pattern.

**Original schematic**:
An explanatory illustration created for the atlas, explicitly distinguished from a published gel image.

**Scientific editor**:
The project owner who approves an entry's scientific interpretation and supporting material before publication.
