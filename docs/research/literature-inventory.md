# VWF multimer literature inventory

Research date: 2026-10-19. Status: six source records verified against article full-text XML; all atlas content remains draft and scientific approval is pending. This is a bounded source inventory, not an approved interpretation of the figures or a complete first-release bibliography.

**First worked example recommendation:** Casonato et al., 2017, Figure 1, patient 10 (type 2B with loss of large multimers). Direct image inspection confirms an authentic gel, labelled normal-plasma lanes and patient numbers, with clinical/hemostatic metadata explicitly linked to Table 1. The article uses CC BY 4.0, with no separate exception found in the caption, XML or inspected image. The [patient 10 extraction draft](examples/casonato-2017-patient-10.md) and retained source figures are ready for scientific editor review before publishing. Heubner Figure 2 is a useful case-linked densitometric trace, **not a gel image**, and should not be substituted for the agreed core visual material.

## Access, evidence, and limitations

Initial requests to PMC, PubMed, E-utilities and Europe PMC failed with proxy CONNECT `403 Forbidden`. After the environment's literature-domain access changed, Europe PMC discovery and full-text XML worked. PMC/PubMed browser article routes returned challenge pages in this research session, so substantive source inspection used Europe PMC's article XML. No challenge was bypassed; TLS verification stayed enabled. Intermittent Europe PMC search `503` responses were isolated by using simpler queries or known DOI lookups rather than repeating identical requests.

The six article identifiers, citations, figures, captions, relevant body passages, tables and XML rights statements below were inspected directly. Their full-text links are the exact endpoints read; PMC figure links are stable human-readable locators, not a claim that this session retrieved an image through that browser route. Direct image inspection was completed for Casonato Figures 1/2, Skornova Figure 3 and Heubner Figure 2, using figure JPEGs from Europe PMC’s `/<PMCID>/supplementaryFiles` ZIP endpoint (which includes main figures as well as supplements). Those main figures were inspected; supplementary documents are not thereby reviewed. Subsequent inspection also covered Focko Figure 2 and Boender Figure 1: Focko patient panel C has curves only (the gel in generic panel B is not patient-linked); Boender A–C include small horizontal gel strips. Vangenechten remains caption/XML-verified without pixel inspection. Final chosen-image resolution, orientation and any credit line require review on the retained asset; do not infer gel content from a caption alone.

Rights assessment here means a documented candidate reuse route, not scientific approval or an unrestricted rights guarantee. Article licenses and figure captions/credit lines in XML were checked for exclusions. Absence of an XML exception does not substitute for checking the actual figure before release. Figures retain their own terms and are excluded from the project's CC0 grant. Use a source link until the intended display is supported. Under NC-ND licenses, do not assume that cropping, adding an annotation overlay, or extracting a panel is permitted; use link-only pending a supported route or separate permission.

Use **not reported** only for relevant inspected source material; **not reviewed** for supplements, image pixels, or sources not inspected. Group statistics must not become patient-level metadata. Publication dates of all six candidates precede the research cutoff; broader discovery used an upper `FIRST_PDATE` bound of 2026-10-19.

## 1. Type 2B with and without large multimers — preferred first example

**Citation:** Casonato A, Daidone V, Galletta E, Bertomoro A. *Type 2B von Willebrand disease with or without large multimers: A distinction of the two sides of the disorder is long overdue.* PLoS ONE. 2017;12(6):e0179566. DOI: `10.1371/journal.pone.0179566`; PMID: `28640903`; PMCID: `PMC5480883`.

**Sources inspected:** [article full-text XML](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC5480883/fullTextXML), Haemostatic tests, Patients, Haemostatic pattern, Tables 1/2, Figures 1/2 captions, permissions. [Figure 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5480883/figure/pone.0179566.g001/) and [Figure 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5480883/figure/pone.0179566.g002/) locators.

- **Figure/case linkage:** Figure 1 caption identifies patient numbers as those in Table 1 (type 2B lacking large multimers). Figure 2 uses Table 2 patient numbers (type 2B with normal multimer pattern). Patient 1 in the two tables is not the same case. Direct image inspection: Figure 1 lanes left to right are NP, 17, 18, NP, 10, 9, NP, 19. Figure 2 lanes are NP, 12, 2, NP, 7, 8, NP, 11. NP labels normal-plasma reference lanes; precise normal-pool composition is not established by these labels. Thus only subsets of the tables are actually shown.
- **Authors' findings:** Figure 1 and Haemostatic pattern report loss of large multimers, reduced intermediate multimers, accumulation of small multimers and stronger satellite bands. Figure 2 represents a normal multimer pattern despite reported type 2B. The paper therefore supports a condition-to-pattern caveat, subject to editor review, rather than a universal rule that type 2B always loses large multimers.
- **Patient/laboratory context:** Tables 1/2 report individual family identifier, age/sex, blood group, platelets, PFA-100, spontaneous aggregation, minimal aggregating ristocetin dose, VWF:Ag, VWF:CB, VWF:RCo and ratios, platelet VWF, FVIII, BAT score and mutation, with units/ranges. Preferred patient 10 in Figure 1 is a 70-year-old female, blood group O, Table 1 family C; table reports platelets 168 × 10^3/µL, VWF:Ag 44.6 U/dL, VWF:CB 10.3 U/dL, VWF:RCo 21.4 U/dL, FVIII 53.2 U/dL, BAT 15 and p.R1308C. Figure 1’s embedded data corroborate antigen/collagen-binding values and mutation. Table 1 provides the remaining assay values/ranges for exact extraction. No invented detailed bleeding history from a BAT score. The Patients section gives group age/sex counts and family structure; those are not lane-specific narratives.
- **Assay/control:** Figure captions specify 1.8% agarose and iodine-125-labelled anti-VWF antibody. Methods gives blood collection/citrate and notes additional inhibitors for platelet-VWF sampling, not automatically for every plasma gel. Some methods refer to earlier work; details not present must remain unresolved until that reference is inspected. Healthy age/sex-matched controls were studied, and inspected figures have repeated NP lanes; exact control composition remains unestablished.
- **Reuse evidence:** Permissions link [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and credit © 2017 Casonato et al. Neither figure's XML/caption contains a third-party exception. No additional credit line is visible in the inspected Figures 1/2 JPEGs. Candidate reproduction with complete attribution, license link and change disclosure; preserve patient labels and normal references in any reviewed presentation.
- **Reason for rank:** Best first actual gel: legible patient-labelled electrophoresis, repeated normal references, metadata in both image and patient-linked table, compatible rights route. Figures 1/2 also support later condition-versus-pattern comparison. Patient 10 is selected specifically rather than treating all cohort patients as pictured.

## 2. Hydrasys single-center inherited VWD examples

**Citation:** Skornova I, Simurda T, Stasko J, et al. *Multimer Analysis of Von Willebrand Factor in Von Willebrand Disease with a Hydrasys Semi-Automatic Analyzer—Single-Center Experience.* Diagnostics. 2021;11(11):2153. DOI: `10.3390/diagnostics11112153`; PMID: `34829500`; PMCID: `PMC8621170`.

**Sources inspected:** [article full-text XML](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC8621170/fullTextXML), Sections 2.1–2.4, Results, Discussion, Tables 1/2, Figure 3 caption, permissions. [Figure 3 locator](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8621170/figure/diagnostics-11-02153-f003/).

- **Figure/case linkage:** Figure 3A is the Hydrasys scan, 3B densitometry. Caption explicitly maps type 1 to Table 1 sample 1, type 2N to sample 2, type 3 to sample 3, and unclassified VWD to sample 4. These sample numbers belong to Table 1; Table 2 has different row ordering. Do not silently treat Table 1 sample 1 as Table 2 sample 1.
- **Pattern/laboratory evidence:** Table 1 provides VWF activity, antigen, activity/antigen ratio, FVIII activity, collagen-binding activity, collagen-binding/antigen ratio, and LMW/IMW/HMW measurements with reference ranges for these four illustrated samples. For example, Table 1 sample 2 is reported as type 2N, with VWF activity 129%, antigen 108%, FVIII 39%, collagen-binding activity 73%, and ratios 1.19 and 0.68. Type 3 is explicitly illustrated, but Discussion states multimers were not detectable while Table 1 contains small nonzero multimer values: preserve this tension for scientific review rather than resolve it by assumption. Other multimer percentage totals also need source-aware review.
- **Clinical context:** Full text provides cohort characteristics and group-level descriptions. Individual history, age, sex, and bleeding presentation tied to each of these four samples are not reported in the inspected text/tables. Do not assign group findings to a sample.
- **Assay/control:** Citrated plasma, fresh or stored at −80°C; healthy-donor pooled control; concentration-dependent dilution; 2% agarose; electrophoresis at 90 V/10 mA for 110 minutes at 15°C; immunofixation with anti-VWF and peroxidase-conjugated antibody; Hydrasys 2/Phoresis analysis (Methods). Source explicitly locates LMW at the top of this gel and HMW at the bottom—do not impose a conventional orientation from another assay. Direct image inspection confirms gel lanes left to right: vWD1, vWD2N, vWD3, vWDU, N. The image labels LMW above HMW; N is the displayed normal reference. Retain the full contextual control rather than cropping to an isolated unlabelled strip. The method does not resolve triplet substructure; avoid using it to teach triplet distinctions.
- **Reuse evidence:** Article permissions: © 2021 authors, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Figure 3 caption/XML has no third-party credit or exception. Figures 1/2 explicitly say own data, but that wording should not be falsely quoted as part of Figure 3. No extra credit is visible in the inspected Figure 3 JPEG. Candidate for attributed reproduction; the available figure is relatively small/soft, so assess teaching legibility before release.
- **Reason for rank:** Several inherited examples with explicit lab linkage and detailed method; weaker patient histories and numerical/text discrepancies make it less convenient than Casonato patient 10 as the first complete worksheet.

## 3. Pediatric ECMO case — complementary trace, no gel

**Citation:** Heubner L, Trautmann-Grill K, Tiebel O, Mirus M, Güldner A, Rand A, Spieth PM. *Treatment of Acquired von Willebrand Disease due to Extracorporeal Membrane Oxygenation in a Pediatric COVID-19 Patient with Vonicog Alfa: A Case Report and Literature Review.* TH Open. 2023;7(1):e76–e81. DOI: `10.1055/a-2008-4367`; PMID: `36846831`; PMCID: `PMC9949976`.

**Sources inspected:** [article full-text XML](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC9949976/fullTextXML), Methods, Case Description, Table 1, Figure 2 caption, permissions. [Figure 2 locator](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9949976/figure/FI22090040-2/).

- **Figure/case linkage:** Figure 2 explicitly compares the patient's multimer distributions under and after ECMO with a healthy control. Caption describes day 3 after bleeding occurred; case body identifies follow-up as one week after decannulation. Directly inspected Figure 2 contains three curves and no gel lanes: purple patient under ECMO, orange patient after ECMO, green healthy control; boxed HMW region at right. It is not eligible as the first authentic-gel example under the agreed scope.
- **Authors' findings:** Nearly complete absence of HMW multimers during ECMO, with reappearance one week after decannulation (Case Description). These are the authors' descriptions, not a new atlas diagnosis.
- **Patient history:** A 16-year-old male with severe COVID-19 respiratory failure requiring ECMO; diffuse GI bleeding followed endoscopic papillotomy for sclerosing cholangitis 47 days after ECMO initiation. Case Description documents sepsis, liver dysfunction, interrupted unfractionated heparin, treatment with recombinant VWF and other interventions, eventual ECMO weaning after 72 days, and ICU discharge on day 110. These multiple time axes must be labeled explicitly.
- **Laboratory context:** Case Description reports VWF:CB/VWF:Ag ratio 0.48 and VWF:GP1bR/VWF:Ag ratio 0.5 before treatment, rising during treatment to 0.64 and 0.82, respectively. Table 1 has daily Hb, platelets, INR, aPTT, FVIII, fibrinogen, D-dimers and anti-Xa, with units/reference ranges. Its VWF:CB `>191%`, VWF:Ag `>400%`, and reported ratio `0.48` occur in the day 4 column; preserve bounds and the reported ratio rather than recomputing from bounds. Do not label these as the same draw as the day 3 multimer trace. FVIII `>400%` is reported on selected days, not every day. Extract each required row directly rather than using flattened text.
- **Assay/control:** Methods names HemosIL AcuStar assays for VWF:Ag, VWF:CB and VWF:GP1bR; Hydragel 5 von Willebrand Multimer kit and a Hydrasis 2 analyzer (source spelling), both Sebia. Figure 2 includes a healthy control. Agarose concentration and a complete gel protocol are not reported in the inspected Methods paragraph; do not borrow them from another Hydrasys paper. Supplementary material is not reviewed.
- **Reuse evidence:** Article permissions identify the authors as rights holders and link [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Figure 2 XML/caption has no third-party credit or exception. Candidate reproduction with attribution, license link and disclosed modifications; No embedded third-party credit was visible in the inspected Figure 2 JPEG; final attribution remains to be prepared. Preserve annotations as a separate layer and keep the figure outside project CC0.
- **Reason for rank:** Strong complementary case-linked trace and longitudinal context, but absence of an actual gel prevents using it as the first core gel example. Avoid suggesting that a single treatment outcome establishes efficacy.

## 4. Heyde syndrome case — verify gel content before inclusion

**Citation:** Focko B, Miertová Z, Škorňová I, et al. *Heyde syndrome: Experiences with the use of semiautomatic vWF multimer analysis for diagnosis and TAVI for treatment – case report.* Medicine (Baltimore). 2025;104(20):e42486. DOI: `10.1097/MD.0000000000042486`; PMID: `40388768`; PMCID: `PMC12091634`.

**Sources inspected:** [article full-text XML](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC12091634/fullTextXML), Case section, Figure 2 caption, permissions. [Figure 2 locator](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12091634/figure/F2/).

- **Exact visual/context:** Figure 2A is a device example, 2B an example study protocol, and 2C the patient's study. Only C is explicitly patient-linked. Caption identifies blue as patient and yellow as control. Direct pixel inspection confirms C has curves only; a gel strip is present in generic B, not the patient-labelled C. Do not use generic B as this patient's gel.
- **Patient history/findings:** A 68-year-old man with repeated symptomatic anemia, prior treated GI bleeding sources, severe paradoxical low-flow/low-gradient aortic stenosis, and author-reported acquired VWF syndrome/Heyde syndrome. Case section reports reduced HMW distribution by Hydrasys 2/densitometry and no symptomatic-anemia readmissions over two years after TAVI. This does not establish post-TAVI gel normalization; no paired follow-up multimer figure is identified.
- **Laboratory context:** Initial Hb 81 g/L, hematocrit 0.27, platelets 196 × 10^9/L. VWF antigen is described as normal; platelet-function testing abnormal and ristocetin-associated binding reduced. Numeric VWF/FVIII/PFA values and their reference intervals are not reported in the inspected case prose. Do not replace qualitative descriptions with invented numbers.
- **Assay/control:** Hydrasys 2 (Sebia); densitometry versus control. Gel concentration, full protocol, and control-pool provenance are not reported in the inspected case section. Generic device photographs/protocols cannot fill these patient-specific gaps.
- **Reuse evidence:** Article permissions state [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), © 2025 authors. Figure 2 XML/caption lists no third-party exception. No separate credit line is visible in the inspected JPEG, but the gel is in a generic panel and does not establish this patient’s image. Use C only as a clearly identified trace.
- **Disposition:** Useful acquired-disease context and case-linked densitometric trace; not a core patient-linked authentic-gel example.

## 5. Reference-range study — broad inherited patterns, NC-ND restriction

**Citation:** Vangenechten I, Gadisseur A. *Improving diagnosis of von Willebrand disease: Reference ranges for von Willebrand factor multimer distribution.* Research and Practice in Thrombosis and Haemostasis. 2020;4(6):1024–1034. DOI: `10.1002/rth2.12408`; PMID: `32864553`; PMCID: `PMC7443431`.

**Sources inspected:** [article full-text XML](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC7443431/fullTextXML), study design, in-house/Hydragel methods, Figures 1/2 captions, permissions. [Figure 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7443431/figure/rth212408-fig-0001/) and [Figure 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7443431/figure/rth212408-fig-0002/) locators.

- **Exact examples:** Figure 1A/B compares Hydragel with in-house gels for UPN85 (type 2M-GPIb) and UPN78 (atypical 2B). Caption reports HMW loss with Hydragel that was not seen with the in-house method. Figure 2 explicitly maps lanes 1/11 normal plasma control, 2 type 1, 3 type 2A/IIA, 4 type 2A/IIC, 5 type 2A/IIE, 6 type 2B, 7 atypical 2B, 8 type 2M-GPIb, 9 type 2N, 10 type 3; no densitometry for lanes 9/10.
- **Metadata cautions:** Figure 2 tables provide group mean percentage AUC and pathological reference intervals, not necessarily the depicted individual's laboratory results. Figure 1 names specific UPNs; individual clinical and laboratory records in supplemental Tables S1–S3 have not been reviewed, so their availability is **not reviewed**, not “not reported.”
- **Assay/control:** Hydragel 5/11 kit on Hydrasys-2 Scan; 2% agarose, antiserum/immunoprecipitation/peroxidase detection and Phoresis v8.63. Normal plasma control on each gel. In-house method uses chemiluminescence with Optigo-750 and TotalLab-100; inspect source protocol before interpreting fine substructure. This figure is valuable precisely because its methods disagree in selected examples.
- **Reuse evidence:** Article permissions identify authors and [CC BY-NC-ND 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/), requiring noncommercial use and no modifications/adaptations. No additional figure-specific credit found in these XML captions. **Link-only for initial atlas planning**; separate permission or another supported rights route is needed before assuming annotated/cropped reuse.

## 6. WiN densitometry study — representative inherited examples, NC-ND restriction

**Citation:** Boender J, Atiq F, Cnossen MH, et al., for the WiN study group. *Von Willebrand Factor Multimer Densitometric Analysis: Validation of the Clinical Accuracy and Clinical Implications in Von Willebrand Disease.* HemaSphere. 2021;5(3):e542. DOI: `10.1097/HS9.0000000000000542`; PMID: `33623884`; PMCID: `PMC7892298`.

**Sources inspected:** [article full-text XML](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC7892298/fullTextXML), Assessment methods, VWF multimer analysis, Densitometric analysis, Characteristics of the study population, Figure 1 caption, Table 1, permissions. [Figure 1 locator](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7892298/figure/F1/).

- **Exact examples:** Characteristics section identifies Figure 1A as type 1 with normal multimers, 1B type 2A with reduced HMW, and 1C type 2B with absent HMW; D explains index calculation. Direct image inspection confirms A–C have small horizontal gel strips beneath the respective densitometric plots. These are representative patients, not an identified single case report.
- **Clinical/laboratory context:** Article reports broad VWF/FVIII assays and bleeding scores in the cohort. Table 1 gives group median/IQR data; it does not establish those values for the illustrated patients. Patient-specific history and numerical case records linked to A–C are not reported in the inspected main article.
- **Assay/control:** 0.9% precast agarose, Western blot with rabbit anti-human VWF polyclonal antibodies, VWF:Ag-normalized application, ImageJ analysis. Medium-large/large ratios are normalized to a normal control on the same Western blot. Definitions of small/medium/large differ from Hydragel peak groupings; do not imply identical quantitative categories across these sources.
- **Reuse evidence:** Author copyright; article explicitly uses [CC BY-NC-ND 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/) and states changes/commercial use require journal permission. Figure 1 XML/caption and inspected JPEG have no separate third-party credit. **Link-only initially**; a free full text is not an annotation license.
