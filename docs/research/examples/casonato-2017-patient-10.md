## Source interpretation versus atlas commentary

### Authors' reported findings

Figure 1 represents patients lacking large VWF multimers. The Results describe a marked reduction in large multimers and changes in intermediate multimers in this group. The patient 10 record supplies p.R1308C and the laboratory values above; individual severity should not be inferred from the darkest band alone.

### Draft teaching explanation — not scientifically approved

Compare patient 10 with the adjacent NP reference. The upper multimer ladder is depleted relative to the normal-plasma lane. Interpret that pattern alongside the reported VWF activity results and patient classification; the gel appearance alone does not establish type 2B VWD.

The same paper provides a useful future companion: Figure 2 shows source-classified type 2B patients with preserved multimer patterns. It supports the atlas's distinction between an observable pattern and an associated condition. These are separate patients; their clinical data must not be attached to patient 10.

The article proposes subgroup terminology and discusses treatment choices. Those proposals are not adopted here as current consensus or treatment advice; evaluating contemporary classification and management would require separate, current evidence.
