DOI: [10.1371/journal.pone.0179566](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0179566). [Article XML](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC5480883/fullTextXML), Figure 1 and Table 1, including the normal-range row and table footnotes, inspected on 2026-10-19.

Only five patient lanes are pictured in Figure 1. Left to right: **NP, 17, 18, NP, 10, 9, NP, 19**. NP lanes are reference plasma, not patient records. Table 1 contains additional patients who are not pictured; they are not added as image examples. Table 2/Figure 2 patient numbers are a separate namespace and are not mixed into these cases.

The table footnote defines **NA as not assessed**; preserve that state, rather than converting it to not reported or zero. Patient-specific clinical histories and exact draw-to-gel timing remain unavailable in the inspected case-linked material. Shared family letters do not establish a patient's individual presentation.

**Patient 19 source discrepancy:** the listed ratios 0.48 and 0.71 do not reconcile arithmetically with antigen 44.7, collagen-binding 11.8, and ristocetin-cofactor 11.4 U/dL. These values are transcribed as published and explicitly flagged in the page for scientific editor review. No corrected value or explanation is invented.
